# Appendix G: Legal Compliance Checklist

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** November 2025  
**Purpose:** Provide comprehensive legal compliance framework for DetourCoin cryptocurrency operations, platform management, and ongoing regulatory obligations.

---

## Executive Summary

This legal compliance checklist addresses the complex regulatory landscape for cryptocurrency-based business platforms. DetourCoin's unique positioning as a utility token within the DetourMarket ecosystem requires careful attention to securities law, money transmitter regulations, tax obligations, consumer protection, and data privacy requirements.

**CRITICAL LEGAL DISCLAIMER:** This checklist provides general compliance guidance and is NOT legal advice. Cryptocurrency regulations are complex, rapidly evolving, and jurisdiction-specific. You MUST engage qualified legal counsel specializing in cryptocurrency and fintech law before launching DetourCoin or the DetourMarket platform. Failure to comply with applicable laws can result in severe civil and criminal penalties.

☐ **Investment of Money**

- Mitigation: Emphasize utility, not investment returns
- Legal Opinion: Required before launch

☐ **Common Enterprise**
- Document: How are DTC holders' fortunes linked?
- Evidence: Tokenomics structure, emission schedule
- Mitigation: Decentralized value creation
- Legal Opinion: Required before launch

☐ **Expectation of Profit**
- Document: How is DTC positioned to users?
- Evidence: All public communications, white paper
- Mitigation: Focus on platform utility, not price appreciation
- Legal Opinion: Required before launch

☐ **Efforts of Others**
- Document: Is profit dependent on promoter efforts?
- Evidence: Management structure, governance model
- Mitigation: Utility-driven, decentralized governance plan
- Legal Opinion: Required before launch

☐ **Ongoing State Compliance**
- Annual renewals
- Financial reporting (quarterly or annual)
- Surety bond renewals
- Change of control notifications
- Examination cooperation

**High-Priority States for Initial Licensing:**
1. California (required, no exemptions for crypto)
2. New York (BitLicense if serving NY customers)
3. Texas (if operating in Texas)
4. Florida (if operating in Florida)
5. Additional states based on merchant locations
