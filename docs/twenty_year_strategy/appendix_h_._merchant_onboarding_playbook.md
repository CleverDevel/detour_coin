## OVERVIEW & PHILOSOPHY

### Core Principles

**1. Value-First Approach**
DetourMarket solves real problems for local businesses. Every interaction should demonstrate tangible value before asking for commitment.

**2. White-Glove to Scale**
Start with high-touch, personal onboarding (Alpha/Beta), then systematically document and automate processes to enable scaling.

**3. Platform Excellence**
Merchant success = platform success = DTC value. The quality of the merchant experience directly impacts long-term ecosystem value.

**4. Zero-Subscription Advantage**
Lead with the revolutionary pricing model: no monthly fees, only pay actual transaction costs. This is the wedge that opens doors.

**5. Community Building**
Merchants aren't customers—they're partners in building a better local economy ecosystem.

### Success Metrics

**Primary KPIs:**
- Merchant acquisition rate (monthly)
- Activation rate (% who complete first transaction within 30 days)
- Retention rate (% active after 90 days, 6 months, 12 months)
- Transaction volume per merchant
- Referral rate (% who refer another merchant)

**Target Benchmarks:**
- **Activation:** >80% within 30 days
- **90-Day Retention:** >85%
- **12-Month Retention:** >75%
- **Referral Rate:** >25% by Year 2

## IDEAL MERCHANT PROFILE

### Primary Target: Multi-Vendor Retail Collectives

**Characteristics:**
- Antique malls, artisan markets, maker spaces
- 10-100+ individual vendors under one roof
- Shared point-of-sale or consignment model
- Annual revenue: $250K-$5M
- Currently using outdated or inefficient systems

**Why They're Ideal:**
- High pain points with existing solutions
- Multiple stakeholders = multiple advocates
- Natural network effects within the community
- Higher transaction volumes
- Strong local presence and tourist appeal

### Secondary Targets

**Category 1: Independent Retailers**
- Boutique shops, specialty stores
- $500K-$2M annual revenue
- Tech-savvy owner/operator
- Limited by current POS systems

**Category 2: Service-Based Businesses**
- Tour operators, experience providers
- Event venues with retail components
- Studios offering classes + merchandise
- $250K-$1M annual revenue

**Category 3: Seasonal/Event Merchants**
- Farmers market vendors
- Festival sellers
- Pop-up shops
- Holiday market participants

### Qualification Criteria

**Must Have:**
1. Annual revenue >$250K (or clear path to it)
2. Physical product sales component
3. Owner/decision-maker accessible
4. Basic technology infrastructure (internet, smartphone/tablet)
5. Interest in improving current systems

**Strong Indicators:**
1. Multiple revenue streams or vendors
2. Tourist/visitor traffic component
3. Active social media presence
4. Participation in local business associations
5. Openness to innovation

**Red Flags:**
1. Locked into long-term contract with competitor
2. Owner resistant to technology
3. Chaotic business operations (suggests poor platform fit)
4. Unrealistic expectations about automation
5. History of churning through vendors/systems

## PRE-ONBOARDING QUALIFICATION

### Step 1: Initial Research (15-30 minutes)

**Before First Contact:**

**Business Intelligence Gathering:**
- [ ] Business name, location, owner name
- [ ] Website and social media presence
- [ ] Approximate size (sq ft, vendor count)
- [ ] Current technology stack (visible systems)
- [ ] Online reviews and reputation
- [ ] Recent news or events
- [ ] Competitive positioning

**Pain Point Hypothesis:**
Based on research, identify likely pain points:
- Inventory management challenges?
- POS limitations?
- Multi-vendor complexity?
- Reporting/analytics gaps?
- Customer engagement issues?

**Value Proposition Customization:**
Prepare 2-3 specific value propositions based on their situation.

6. "What do you love about your current setup?"
7. "What frustrates you most about your current systems?"

9. "How comfortable are you with learning new software?"
10. "Do you have reliable internet at your location?"
11. "What devices do you use daily? (smartphone, tablet, computer)"

12. "Who else would need to be involved in evaluating new systems?"
13. "When does your current contract/commitment expire?"
14. "What would make you consider switching systems?"

**Business Goals:**
15. "Where do you want your business to be in 12 months?"
16. "What would 'success' with a new platform look like?"

## SALES & ENROLLMENT PROCESS

### Phase 1: Initial Contact & Discovery

**Goal:** Establish relationship, understand needs, qualify opportunity

**Outreach Channels (in priority order):**

**1. Warm Referrals (Best):**
- Introduction from existing merchant
- Mutual connection in business community
- Industry association referral

**Template Email (Referral):**
```
Subject: [Referrer Name] suggested we connect

Hi [Name],

[Referrer Name] mentioned you might be interested in learning 
about DetourMarket—a new platform specifically built for 
[their business type].

We're helping businesses like [Their Business] eliminate 
subscription fees while getting better inventory management 
and analytics. [Referrer] saved [X hours/week or $X/month] 
after switching.

Would you be open to a brief conversation? I'd love to learn 
about [Their Business] and see if there's a fit.

Best,
Joe
[Contact Info]
```

**2. Direct Outreach (Trade Shows, Local Events):**
- In-person introduction at industry events
- Booth visits at relevant conferences
- Local business networking events

**In-Person Opening:**
```
"Hi, I'm Joe. I noticed you're running a [business type]—
I built a platform specifically for businesses like yours 
that eliminates monthly software fees. Would you be open 
to seeing a quick demo, or can I leave you some information?"
```

**3. Cold Outreach (Strategic):**
- Targeted emails to ideal profile businesses
- LinkedIn messages to owners
- Direct mail for high-value targets

**Cold Email Template:**
```
Subject: Eliminate your POS subscription fees

Hi [Name],

I'm reaching out because [Their Business] looks like exactly 
the type of business DetourMarket was built to serve.

Quick question: How much do you currently pay monthly for 
your POS and inventory management system?

Most [business type] owners we work with were paying $150-300/month. 
With DetourMarket, they pay zero monthly fees—just actual 
transaction costs.

Interested in learning how this works?

Joe
[Contact Info]
```

### Phase 2: Discovery Call (30-45 minutes)

**Goal:** Deeply understand their business, build relationship, demonstrate value

**Call Structure:**

**Opening (5 minutes):**
1. Warm introduction and rapport building
2. Set agenda: "I'd like to learn about your business, show you what we've built, and see if there's a good fit."
3. Get permission to take notes

**Discovery (15-20 minutes):**

Use qualification questions (see above) conversationally. Focus on:
- Current workflows and pain points
- Time spent on manual tasks
- Vendor/inventory complexity
- Reporting and analytics needs
- Customer experience considerations
- Financial impact of current systems

**Listen for gold nuggets:**
- "I wish I could..."
- "It's so frustrating when..."
- "I spend way too much time on..."
- "I have no visibility into..."

**Tailored Demonstration (15-20 minutes):**

**Do NOT give a generic demo.** Show them solutions to THEIR specific problems.

**Demo Framework:**
1. "Based on what you've told me, let me show you how DetourMarket handles [specific pain point]..."
2. Walk through their exact use case
3. Highlight zero-subscription model
4. Show time-saving features for their workflows
5. Demonstrate reporting they currently lack

**Demo Flow:**
- **Problem 1 → Solution:** "You mentioned [pain]. Here's how we solve that..."
- **Problem 2 → Solution:** "You said [frustration]. Watch this..."
- **Problem 3 → Solution:** "For [challenge], we built..."

**Closing (5 minutes):**

Ask directly: "Does this look like it would solve the problems you're facing?"

**If YES:**
"Great! Here's what happens next. I'll send you a detailed implementation plan and we can schedule your onboarding. The whole process typically takes 2-3 weeks. Does that timeline work for you?"

**If HESITATION:**
"What questions do you have?" or "What would you need to see to move forward?"

**If NO:**
"I appreciate your time. What would make this a better fit?" (gather feedback)

**Attachments:**
- Detailed implementation timeline
- Platform overview (1-2 pages)
- Case study or testimonial (if available)
- Simple terms of service

### Phase 4: Contract & Commitment

**Keep It Simple:**

For Alpha/Beta phase:
- Simple 1-2 page agreement
- No long-term lock-in
- Clear expectations on both sides
- Signature via DocuSign or similar

**Key Terms:**
1. Service description
2. Pricing structure (zero subscription)
3. Data ownership (merchant owns their data)
4. Support commitments
5. Termination terms (either party, 30 days notice)
6. Beta participation expectations (feedback, patience)

**Red Line Issues (Non-Negotiable):**
- Merchant data ownership and portability
- No platform fee beyond gas costs
- Merchant's right to terminate
- Platform's right to refuse service

## TECHNICAL ONBOARDING

### Pre-Onboarding Preparation (Before Kick-Off Call)

**Merchant Homework (Send 72 Hours Before):**

**Email Template:**
```
Subject: Get Ready for DetourMarket - Action Items

Hi [Name],

Excited to get you onboarded! To make our kick-off call 
productive, please gather the following:

REQUIRED:
□ Current inventory list (Excel/CSV format preferred)
□ Vendor information (if multi-vendor)
□ Product categories/taxonomies you use
□ Current POS system login (if applicable)

OPTIONAL BUT HELPFUL:
□ Sample sales reports from current system
□ List of custom fields you track
□ Your logo and brand colors
□ Photos of top-selling products

Don't worry if everything isn't perfect—we can work through 
it together!

See you on [Date] at [Time]!

Joe
```

**Your Preparation:**
- [ ] Create merchant account in platform
- [ ] Prepare custom onboarding checklist
- [ ] Review their business profile
- [ ] Test environment setup
- [ ] Prepare training materials

### Kick-Off Call (60-90 minutes)

**Agenda:**

**1. Welcome and Orientation (10 minutes)**
- Reconfirm what they'll accomplish today
- Set expectations for timeline
- Introduce support resources

**2. Account Setup (15 minutes)**
- Create login credentials
- Walk through dashboard overview
- Set up user permissions (if multiple staff)
- Configure basic settings

**3. Data Import Strategy (20 minutes)**

**Option A: CSV Upload (Most Common)**
```
Step 1: Review their inventory file
Step 2: Map columns to platform fields
Step 3: Clean obvious data issues
Step 4: Upload and validate
Step 5: Spot-check sample records
```

**Option B: Manual Entry (Small Catalogs)**
```
Step 1: Create product categories
Step 2: Add 5-10 sample products together
Step 3: Assign them to complete the rest
Step 4: Schedule review call
```

**Option C: API Integration (Advanced)**
```
Step 1: Exchange API credentials
Step 2: Configure data sync settings
Step 3: Run test import
Step 4: Set up automated sync schedule
```

**4. Multi-Vendor Setup (If Applicable) (15 minutes)**
- Create vendor accounts
- Set commission structures
- Configure vendor-specific permissions
- Explain vendor portal access

**5. Product Organization (15 minutes)**
- Set up categories and tags
- Configure product attributes
- Upload product images (show process)
- Set pricing strategies

**6. POS Integration (15 minutes)**
- Connect to existing hardware (if compatible)
- Configure payment processing
- Set up receipt printing
- Test a sample transaction

**7. Next Steps and Schedule (10 minutes)**
- Assign "homework" (complete data import)
- Schedule training session (within 7 days)
- Provide emergency contact info
- Answer questions

**Closing:**
"You're going to do great! Remember, I'm here to help. If you get stuck on anything, just reach out. I'll check in with you in 3 days to see how the data import is going."

### Data Import & Configuration (Week 1-2)

**Merchant's Work (Async):**
- Complete inventory import
- Upload product images
- Configure categories and tags
- Add vendor information
- Set up tax rates

**Your Work:**
- Monitor progress daily
- Answer questions promptly (within 4 hours)
- Spot-check data quality
- Flag issues proactively
- Prepare for training session

**Quality Checkpoints:**
- [ ] 80%+ of inventory imported
- [ ] Product images on top SKUs
- [ ] Categories properly structured
- [ ] Vendor accounts configured (if applicable)
- [ ] Pricing verified

## TRAINING & ACTIVATION

### Training Session 1: Daily Operations (90 minutes)

**Goal:** Merchant can confidently run their business on DetourMarket

**Session Structure:**

**1. POS Workflow (30 minutes)**

**Live Practice:**
- Ring up a sale (simple)
- Ring up a sale (complex: multiple items, discounts)
- Process return
- Handle special orders
- End-of-day procedures

**Have them do it:** Watch them complete 3-5 transactions solo.

**2. Inventory Management (30 minutes)**

**Live Practice:**
- Add new product
- Update pricing
- Adjust inventory counts
- Manage variants
- Bulk operations

**Have them do it:** Add 3 new products independently.

**3. Reporting & Analytics (20 minutes)**

**Show them:**
- Daily sales summary
- Best-selling products
- Vendor performance (if applicable)
- Inventory valuation
- Custom reports

**Have them do it:** Pull yesterday's sales report.

**4. Mobile Access (10 minutes)**

**Demonstrate:**
- Mobile app navigation
- Quick inventory checks
- On-the-go sales entry
- Photo uploads

**Closing Checklist:**
- [ ] Can complete sale independently
- [ ] Can add/edit products
- [ ] Can pull basic reports
- [ ] Knows where to get help
- [ ] Comfortable with mobile app

### Training Session 2: Advanced Features (60 minutes)

**Scheduled 1-2 Weeks After Session 1**

**Topics Based on Their Needs:**
- Multi-location management
- Advanced reporting and analytics
- Vendor portal administration
- Customer database management
- Loyalty program configuration
- Integrations (accounting, e-commerce)
- Bulk operations and data management

**Format:**
- Quick review of basics
- Deep dive on 2-3 advanced topics
- Q&A on real scenarios they've encountered
- Preview of upcoming features

**Before Flipping the Switch:**

**Technical Readiness:**
- [ ] All critical inventory imported
- [ ] POS hardware configured and tested
- [ ] Payment processing active
- [ ] Receipt printer working
- [ ] Staff accounts created
- [ ] Backup/redundancy plan in place

**Operational Readiness:**
- [ ] Team trained on core workflows
- [ ] Testing completed successfully
- [ ] Old system backup exported
- [ ] Cutover plan documented
- [ ] Customer communication prepared

**Support Readiness:**
- [ ] Emergency contact info shared
- [ ] First 48-hour support plan
- [ ] FAQ document provided
- [ ] Video tutorials accessible
- [ ] Backup contact (if you're unavailable)

**Go-Live Day:**
- Be available via phone for the entire business day
- Check in at opening, mid-day, and closing
- Monitor system for any issues
- Celebrate their success!

## REFERRAL PROGRAM

### Philosophy

The best merchant acquisition channel is happy merchants. Design the program to reward advocacy naturally.

### Program Structure

**Tier 1: Organic Referrals (Always Active)**

**Merchant Benefits:**
- $100 credit per qualified referral who activates
- Recognition in monthly newsletter
- "Top Advocate" badge in merchant directory
- Early access to new features

**Referred Merchant Benefits:**
- Expedited onboarding
- Extended white-glove support (45 days instead of 30)
- "Referral Partner" discount on any paid features

**How It Works:**
1. Merchant refers friend/colleague via unique link
2. Referral mentions them during signup
3. Referred merchant completes onboarding and 10 transactions
4. Both receive benefits

**Tier 2: Structured Advocate Program (Invite-Only)**

**Criteria for Invitation:**
- 6+ months active
- High engagement (top 25% transaction volume)
- 2+ successful referrals
- Positive NPS (9-10)

**Advocate Benefits:**
- $250 per qualified referral
- Quarterly advocate summit (virtual)
- Input on product roadmap
- Co-marketing opportunities
- Revenue share on referred merchant's transactions (0.5% for 12 months)

**Advocate Responsibilities:**
- Participate in case studies
- Provide product feedback
- Speak at events or webinars
- Test new features pre-launch

### Referral Activation Tactics

**Make It Easy:**
- One-click referral link generation in dashboard
- Pre-written email templates
- Social media share buttons
- Printable referral cards for in-person

**Make It Visible:**
- Monthly "Top Referrers" leaderboard
- Success stories featuring advocates
- Showcase referral bonuses earned

**Make It Compelling:**
- "Help a fellow business owner save $200/month"
- "Build a stronger local business community"
- "Earn rewards while doing good"

## PHASE-SPECIFIC STRATEGIES

### Alpha Phase (Months 1-3, Target: 25 Merchants)

**Focus:** Learn, iterate, build relationships

**Merchant Selection:**
- Personal network only
- Forgiving personalities
- Diverse business types (for learning)
- Willing to provide candid feedback

**Onboarding Approach:**
- 100% white-glove
- Weekly check-in calls
- In-person setup preferred
- Unlimited support
- Free everything

**Success Criteria:**
- All 25 merchants active
- <10% churn
- 3+ major feature improvements
- NPS >50
- 5+ testimonials collected

### Beta Phase (Months 4-6, Target: 50 Additional = 75 Total)

**Focus:** Refine process, expand geographically

**Merchant Selection:**
- 50% referrals from alpha merchants
- 50% targeted outreach in new markets
- More stringent qualification
- Start charging minimal fees if applicable

**Onboarding Approach:**
- Semi-automated (documented processes)
- Group training sessions (5-10 merchants)
- First 30 days white-glove, then standard support
- Record all training for future use

**Success Criteria:**
- 90%+ activation rate
- <15% churn
- 2+ case studies published
- 20% referral rate
- Documented onboarding playbook

### Early Scaling (Months 7-12, Target: 175 Additional = 250 Total)

**Focus:** Scale systems, prove unit economics

**Merchant Selection:**
- 60% referrals
- 40% marketing-driven (ads, content, events)
- Strong qualification (score >40)
- Geographic clustering for efficiency

**Onboarding Approach:**
- Primarily self-serve with support
- Recorded training videos
- Group webinars (weekly)
- 1:1 support for high-value merchants only

**Success Criteria:**
- 85%+ activation
- <20% churn
- Positive unit economics
- 30% referral rate
- Self-serve onboarding option launched

### Growth Phase (Year 2-5, Scale to 3,500+ Merchants)

**Focus:** Systematic acquisition, operational excellence

**Merchant Selection:**
- Data-driven lead scoring
- Marketing automation
- Sales team (if applicable)
- Multiple acquisition channels

**Onboarding Approach:**
- Fully automated self-serve
- AI-assisted chatbot support
- Office hours for live help
- Merchant community forum

## TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

### Common Onboarding Challenges

**1. Merchant Overwhelmed by Data Import**

**Symptoms:**
- Stalled after kickoff call
- No progress in 7+ days
- Emails asking how to do "everything"

**Solutions:**
- Schedule working session: "Let's spend an hour together and knock this out"
- Offer to import data for them (if feasible)
- Break into smaller chunks: "Just get your top 50 products in first"
- Provide template spreadsheet with sample data

**2. Low Technical Proficiency**

**Symptoms:**
- Struggles with basic navigation
- Multiple support tickets on simple tasks
- Fear/anxiety about the system

**Solutions:**
- Extended white-glove period (60-90 days)
- In-person training if geographically feasible
- Assign "tech buddy" from their staff
- Create custom video walkthroughs
- Consider if they're truly a fit

**3. Integration Issues**

**Symptoms:**
- Existing hardware won't connect
- Payment processing errors
- Data sync failures

**Solutions:**
- Test environment troubleshooting
- Hardware compatibility check (before onboarding ideally)
- Alternative integration paths
- Temporary workarounds while resolving
- Clear communication about limitations

**4. Buyer's Remorse**

**Symptoms:**
- Sudden ghosting after commitment
- Delays in starting
- Questions about "going back" to old system

**Solutions:**
- Acknowledge the fear: "Change is hard. This is normal."
- Reinforce value proposition: "Remember why you wanted to switch?"
- Offer gradual transition: "Run both systems for 2 weeks"
- Share success stories from similar merchants
- Worst case: "You can always go back—no long-term commitment"

**5. Staff Resistance**

**Symptoms:**
- Owner engaged, staff not
- Complaints about "old system was better"
- Workarounds instead of using platform

**Solutions:**
- Include staff in training (not just owner)
- Identify staff champions and empower them
- Gamification/incentives for adoption
- Address specific concerns individually
- Owner sets expectations and accountability

### Red Flags to Decline/Pause Onboarding

**Ethical Concerns:**
- Questionable business practices
- Hostile or abusive behavior
- Requests to hide information or evade regulations

**Poor Fit:**
- Unrealistic expectations despite clear communication
- Unwilling to invest any time in learning
- Wants completely custom solution (not platform fit)
- Needs critical features we don't have (and won't build)

**Operational Risk:**
- Chaotic business with no systems
- Owner unreachable or unresponsive
- Extreme support demands during trial
- History of litigation against vendors

**How to Decline Gracefully:**
```
"After learning more about [Business], I don't think 
DetourMarket is the right fit at this time. 

[Specific reason without being harsh]

I'd recommend exploring [alternative solution]. 

Happy to revisit this conversation in [timeframe] if 
circumstances change. I wish you all the best."
```
